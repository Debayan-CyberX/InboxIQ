# Workspace visual integrations

The authenticated workspace uses `@shadergradient/react` by ruucm and React Three Fiber by pmndrs under their MIT licenses. Their package license notices remain in the installed distributions.

## Liquid Glass JS

Source: https://github.com/dashersw/liquid-glass-js

The dock adapts the capsule-distance and edge/rim refraction approach to sample only InboxIQ's own backdrop canvas, with a smaller blur kernel and explicit GPU cleanup.

Source: https://github.com/collidingScopes/liquid-logo
